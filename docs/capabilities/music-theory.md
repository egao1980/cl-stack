# music-theory (P2): pitch, interval, key, meter, chord symbols

**Issues:** open: [music-theory#3](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/3) (MT1 follow-ups), [#11](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/11) and [#13](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/13) (logic migration, [below](#logic-migration)), [#14](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/14) (spelling of chromatic steps)
**Status:** **shipped**: slices MT0–MT4 done, [`egao1980/music-theory`](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory) OCI **0.5.0** published
**Repos:** `music-theory` (nick `stack-music`)
**Builds on:** nothing. Pure CL with exact rational arithmetic and no dependencies. MT4 evaluated [music-spelling](https://github.com/ntrocado/music-spelling) (Apache-2.0) for an unmodified import and rejected it: nothing is imported ([MT4](#mt4-spelling-key-detection-transposition-050-done)).

The vocabulary of tonal music theory as **values and arithmetic**. Its consumers are [score-protocol](score.md), MIDI import, audio transcription and OMR ([omr.md](omr.md)), engraving ([engrave.md](engrave.md)), and any [logic-protocol](logic.md) program that reasons about chords, keys or voice-leading.

The library does no I/O and holds no rules or style decisions. It answers questions such as "which spelled pitch classes does `G7/B` realise", "what interval lies between E♭4 and A4", "how many beats fit in 9.5 s at ♩ = 72 in 6/8", and "which key does this pitch histogram suggest".

## Locked decisions

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **Values** | Immutable structs, comparable with `equalp`, printable readably. No CLOS state. | They get hashed, interned in FSet maps, and used as Datalog terms by consumers |
| **Pitch** | `spelled-pitch` = `letter` (0–6, C…B) + `alter` (integer −2…+2; rational microtones reserved) + `octave` (scientific: C4 = MIDI 60). `pitch-midi` is derived; `pitch-class` is unspelled 0–11. | Spelling is information (F♯ ≠ G♭), MIDI loses it |
| **Interval** | `interval` = `steps` (diatonic, signed, compound kept) + `semitones`. The quality (P M m A d, AA dd) is derived. `simple-interval` reduces to within an octave; `interval-between` and `transpose-pitch` are exact. | P12 ≠ P5 for spacing rules; quality is never stored twice |
| **Line of fifths** | Every spelled pitch class has an integer `fifths` position (C 0, G 1, F −1, F♯ 6, B♭ −2). Keys, spelling and enharmonic choice are all measured on it. | One metric for spelling, key distance and transposition |
| **Time** | Durations and positions are **CL rationals in whole notes** (1/4 = a quarter). Never floats. Tuplets are a rational ratio; dots are derived (`duration-dots`, `split-duration` into notatable tied pieces). | Exact sums for measure fill and ties |
| **Meter** | `meter`: numerator(s) (additive: `(2 3)/8`) and denominator; `beat-unit` and `beats` (compound 6/8 = 2 dotted quarters); `metric-weight meter offset` gives an integer level (0 = downbeat; higher = weaker). | Rules ask "strong beat?"; quantisers and spellers need beat grids |
| **Tempo arithmetic** | `tempo` = beats per minute + beat unit. `duration-seconds` and `seconds-duration` convert between rational durations and seconds. **Phrase-window arithmetic:** `max-window-duration tempo seconds` gives the longest rational span that fits a time budget. Tempo *maps* belong to score-protocol. | Consumers that need "a rest at least every N seconds" or "≤ N seconds per phrase" share one exact conversion |
| **Key** | `key` = tonic (spelled pitch class) + `mode` (`:major` `:minor`; church modes reserved) → `key-signature` (fifths −7…+7), `scale-degrees`, `degree-of pitch key` (→ `^1`…`^7` with an alteration), minor-scale variants (natural, harmonic, melodic). | — |
| **Chord symbols** | `parse-chord-symbol text &key dialect` gives a `chord-symbol`: root, quality, extensions (9 11 13) with alterations (♭5 ♯5 ♭9 ♯9 ♯11 ♭13), `sus2` / `sus4`, `add`, `omit`, power chord `5`, `alt`, slash bass (which may be outside the chord), `:no-chord` (N.C.). The **grammar is closed and documented as EBNF in the repo**, and parsed by a hand-written recursive descent. No regex. | No CL chord-symbol parser exists (survey 2026-11) |
| **Dialects** | `:classical`, `:pop` and `:jazz` decide ambiguous spellings: `dim` = triad (classical) or dim7 (pop/jazz); `-` = minor; `Δ` = maj7; `ø` = m7♭5; `+` = aug; `7alt`. `print-chord-symbol` prints in a chosen dialect. | Lead sheets differ by tradition, and the reader must be told which one applies |
| **Realisation** | `chord-tones chord` gives `(degree . spelled-pitch-class)` pairs (1 3 5 7 9 11 13, with alterations). `chord-essential-tones chord n` returns the n most important tones under a **per-quality priority list** (e.g. dominant 13: 3, 7, 13, then root, 9, 5; 11 is omitted). The list is data in the repo, one row per quality. | Consumers with fewer voices than chord tones need a principled choice; the priorities are data, not branches |
| **Roman numerals** | `roman-numeral` = degree + quality + inversion figure + applied target (V/V) + mixture flag. `roman-for chord key` and `chord-for roman key` convert both ways. | Data conversion only; harmonic *analysis* belongs to consumers |
| **Spelling** | `spell-midi midi &key key previous` (one note) and `spell-sequence midis &key key (window 8)`. Line-of-fifths rules S0–S7, the first that applies: previous note, key (a minor key's raised 7th included), one side, the window's accidental, semitone resolution, no augmented or diminished step, nearest center, and a tie signals `ambiguous-spelling` (restarts `use-value`, `muffle-warning`). Returns spelled pitches plus a per-note ambiguity d/6 (distance from the center). With no key, `spell-sequence` uses the first key of `detect-key` on note counts. | MIDI and audio import, transposition |
| **Key detection** | `detect-key histogram &key profiles` returns all 24 keys as `(key . correlation)`, best first (Krumhansl–Kessler by default; Temperley, Aarden selectable, or a caller's `(major minor)` pair). The input is a duration-weighted pitch-class histogram (`pitch-class-histogram`). Pearson r is computed in rationals and the ranking decided exactly; ties keep a fixed candidate order; a constant histogram gives `NIL`. The candidates are spelled with the fewest accidentals (D♭ major, C♯ minor, F♯ major, E♭ minor). | The score is a confidence a UI can flag |
| **Transposition** | `transpose-pitch p interval` preserves spelling (F♯ up M2 is G♯). `transpose-to-key p from to` is key-aware respelling. `choose-enharmonic-key key` prefers ≤ 6 accidentals (D♭ over C♯ major), with an override. Chord symbols transpose and respell the same way. | One transposition semantics for notes and symbols |
| **No rules here** | No voice-leading, doubling or style logic. Those are logic-protocol programs in consumer code (see [logic.md](logic.md), rules-and-logic). Tables in this repo are **facts** (interval names, chord qualities, priorities, profiles). Two rule sets inside the library, chord-symbol admission and spelling, are still hand-written and tracked for migration ([below](#logic-migration)). | Keeps the logic-family policy |

## Protocol surface

```lisp
(defstruct spelled-pitch letter alter octave)         ; (make-spelled-pitch :letter 3 :alter 1 :octave 4) = F♯4
(defstruct pitch-class-name letter alter)             ; spelled, octave-free
(defstruct interval steps semitones)
(defstruct meter numerators denominator)
(defstruct tempo bpm beat-unit)
(defstruct key tonic mode)
(defstruct chord-symbol root quality extensions alterations suspension
           added omitted bass no-chord-p text dialect)
(defstruct roman-numeral degree quality figure applied-to mixture-p)

(defun parse-pitch (string))  (defun pitch-name (pitch))       ; "Eb4" ↔ pitch
(defun pitch-midi (pitch))    (defun pitch-fifths (pitch-or-class))
(defun interval-between (p q)) (defun transpose-pitch (p interval))
(defun interval-quality (interval)) (defun simple-interval (interval))
(defun parse-interval (string)) (defun interval-name (interval)) ; "m3", "P12", "A4"

(defun duration-dots (duration)) (defun split-duration (duration &key meter offset))
(defun meter-beats (meter))  (defun beat-unit (meter))  (defun compound-p (meter))
(defun metric-weight (meter offset))                   ; → 0 downbeat, larger = weaker
(defun duration-seconds (duration tempo)) (defun seconds-duration (seconds tempo))
(defun max-window-duration (tempo seconds &key grid))   ; phrase-window arithmetic

(defun key-signature (key)) (defun scale-degrees (key &key variant))
(defun degree-of (pitch key)) (defun pitch-of-degree (degree key &key octave))

(defun parse-chord-symbol (text &key (dialect :pop)))
(defun print-chord-symbol (chord &key dialect))
(defun chord-tones (chord))                            ; → ((1 . C) (3 . E) (5 . G) (7 . B♭))
(defun chord-essential-tones (chord n))
(defun roman-for (chord key)) (defun chord-for (roman key))

(defun spell-midi (midi &key key previous))
(defun spell-sequence (midis &key key (window 8)))      ; → spelled pitches, ambiguity scores
(defun pitch-class-histogram (events &key (weight :duration))) ; events: (pitch . duration) or bare pitches
(defun detect-key (histogram &key (profiles :krumhansl-kessler)))
(defun transpose-to-key (pitch from-key to-key))
(defun transpose-chord-symbol (chord interval-or-keys))
(defun choose-enharmonic-key (key &key (max-accidentals 6) prefer)) ; prefer :sharps, :flats or NIL
```

### Conditions

```text
music-theory-error (error)
├── invalid-pitch                ; slots: text | letter alter octave
├── invalid-interval
├── invalid-meter
├── invalid-key
└── chord-symbol-parse-error     ; slots: text, position, expected — restarts use-value (a chord-symbol), skip-chord-symbol

├── unknown-chord-quality        ; parsed with a fallback quality — restart use-value
└── ambiguous-spelling           ; slots: midi, candidates — muffled by default
```

## Slices

Each slice is one team-solve run against a contract branch in `music-theory`. All five are done, and 0.5.0 is published.

### MT0: pitch, interval, line of fifths (0.1.0, done)

**Given:** package and exports, structs, conditions, the interval-name table. **Stubbed:** parsing and printing pitches and intervals, `interval-between`, `transpose-pitch`, quality, `pitch-fifths`. **Fixtures:** every letter × alter (−2…+2) × octave 0–8 against music21-generated MIDI and fifths values; all intervals between 35 spelled classes across two octaves. **Tests:** round trips (`parse` ∘ `print` = id); transpose ∘ inverse = id; compound intervals; enharmonic inequality; `chord-symbol-parse-error` is not reachable from pitch parsing.

### MT1: time, meter, tempo, phrase windows (0.2.0, done)

**Given:** `meter` and `tempo` structs. **Stubbed:** `duration-dots`, `split-duration` (tie splitting at beat and bar boundaries, Gould rules), `metric-weight`, compound detection, the seconds conversions, `max-window-duration`. **Fixtures:** 4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 9/8, 12/8, 2/2, 5/4, `(2 3)/8`; tuplet ratios 3:2, 5:4. **Tests:** sums are exact; split pieces re-sum to the input; `metric-weight` ordering per meter; seconds ↔ duration round trip at tempos 40–200.

### MT2: keys, scales, degrees, Roman numerals (0.3.0, done)

**Stubbed:** `key-signature`, `scale-degrees` (natural, harmonic, melodic minor), `degree-of`, `pitch-of-degree`, `roman-for` / `chord-for` with inversions and applied chords. **Fixtures:** all 30 major and minor keys (fifths −7…+7); 60 Roman-numeral ↔ chord pairs generated with music21. **Tests:** degree ∘ pitch-of-degree = id; applied chords (V/V, vii°7/V); mixture (♭VI in major).

### MT3: chord-symbol grammar, dialects, realisation, priorities (0.4.0, done)

**Given:** the EBNF and the quality and priority tables (data). **Stubbed:** the parser, the printer, `chord-tones`, `chord-essential-tones`, `transpose-chord-symbol`. **Fixtures:** about 300 symbols covering every MusicXML `<kind>` value, extensions and alterations, sus/add/omit, power chords, slash bass inside and outside the chord, N.C., Unicode ♯/♭/Δ/ø/°, and dialect-ambiguous cases (`Cdim` under each dialect). **Tests:** parse → print → parse = id per dialect; realised tones against vectors; essential-tone priority for n = 3, 4; `chord-symbol-parse-error` carries a position and its restarts work. A gap found after release, [#10](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/10) (a diminished triad or half-diminished seventh without its 5 printed as `Cm(b5,omit5)`, which R5 rejected on reading), is fixed in 0.5.0: R5 accepts a written ♭5 with omit5 when the flat fifth names the quality.

### MT4: spelling, key detection, transposition (0.5.0, done)

**First task:** evaluate [music-spelling](https://github.com/ntrocado/music-spelling) against MT4's tests. If its penalties express key-aware spelling unmodified, import it (`cl-stack-systems`) and wrap it; otherwise implement line-of-fifths spelling. Record the decision here. **Stubbed:** `spell-midi`, `spell-sequence`, `pitch-class-histogram`, `detect-key`, `transpose-to-key`, `choose-enharmonic-key`. **Fixtures:** 50 public-domain melodies (re-encoded by us) with their notated spelling and key; Krumhansl–Kessler, Temperley and Aarden profile tables (published numbers, cited). **Tests:** spelling accuracy ≥ 98 % on the melodies with a key hint and ≥ 95 % without; key detection at music21's own counts per profile (calibrated, below; the first target was top-1 ≥ 85 % and top-2 ≥ 95 %); transposition respells chord symbols; enharmonic-key choice.

**Done (0.5.0).**

- **music-spelling: evaluated and rejected, nothing imported.** Loaded unmodified (commit `c158400`) and run on the 50 melodies, it spells 97.94 % of the notes with the key as its starting accidental table, 96.35 % without, and 92.05 % in the remote keys of the 100 transposed copies. It ignores the key for a single note (61 in D♭ major is C♯), since a one-note block returns its first candidate whatever the penalties; it carries a key only in its first block; it returns no per-note score; it keeps global state (a parsimony closure, and a memo table that grows with every call); and it fails with a `type-error` when the last block holds one note. Its penalties cannot express key-aware spelling unmodified, so MT4 specifies its own line-of-fifths rules S0–S7 (`src/spell.lisp`). They spell all 1699 notes of the 50-tune corpus with and without the key, and all 3398 of the transposed copies with the key (90.41 % without, where detection cannot tell F♯ major from G♭ major). The corpus was in view while the rules were written, so these figures are in-sample. A gap outside the corpus: [#14](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/14), chromatic steps spelled far from the key (F♭ in the lament bass G F♯ F E E♭ D in G minor).
- **Key-detection targets, calibrated.** `detect-key` reproduces music21's rankings exactly, so the sealed tests hold each profile to music21's own top-1 / top-2 counts over the 50 melodies: Krumhansl–Kessler **39/47**, Temperley **45/49**, Aarden **41/48**. The earlier target, top-1 ≥ 85 % and top-2 ≥ 95 %, is met only by Temperley (90 % / 98 %). Krumhansl–Kessler (78 % / 94 %) and Aarden (82 % / 96 %) miss it on short monophonic melodies: the relative minor, the dominant or subdominant key, the parallel mode, a Dorian tune.
- **Transposition.** `transpose-to-key` moves the tonic's letter at most three steps and keeps degree and alteration, so moving back is the exact inverse, and the roots and basses of `transpose-chord-symbol` with two keys are those of `transpose-to-key`. `choose-enharmonic-key` works on signatures: D♭ over C♯ major by default, F♯ and G♭ major kept as given, `:prefer` and `:max-accidentals` override.

## Logic migration

The logic-family policy ([logic.md](logic.md)) covers rules, precedence and selection. Two rule sets in music-theory are still hand-written first-match `cond` chains, each kept in one function so that the move stays local:

- **Chord-symbol admission rules R1–R6** (`%admit-item`, `src/chord-symbol.lisp`): one suspension, on a quality with a major third; a 5 altered only where the fifth is perfect; no repeated alteration; no added tone already in the chord; an omitted tone must be a natural chord tone; `alt` only on a plain dominant seventh. [music-theory#11](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/11).
- **Spelling rules S0–S7** (`%spell-choice`, `src/spell.lisp`): first-match precedence over the two or three spellings of a MIDI number. [music-theory#13](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/13). The fix for [#14](https://github.com/egao1980/music-theory/issues/14) changes the same rules.

## Non-goals (wave 1)

- Microtones, tunings and temperaments (`alter` rationals are reserved)
- Non-Western pitch systems
- Harmonic analysis, cadence detection and voice-leading rules (consumer logic programs)
- Any file format, MIDI or audio (see [score.md](score.md), [audio.md](audio.md))
